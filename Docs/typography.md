# Typography Guidelines

Typography rules, font families, and responsive scale:

- **Font Family:** `Outfit, Inter, -apple-system, sans-serif`
- **Monospace Font:** `Fira Code, JetBrains Mono, monospace`

## Scale & Sizes
- **Display / H1:** 3.5rem (56px) — Bold (700)
- **H2:** 2.5rem (40px) — Semi-Bold (600)
- **H3:** 1.75rem (28px) — Semi-Bold (600)
- **Body Large:** 1.125rem (18px) — Regular (400)
- **Body Base:** 1rem (16px) — Regular (400)
- **Buttons & Nav:** 0.875rem (14px) — Medium (500)
- **Captions:** 0.75rem (12px) — Regular (400)
