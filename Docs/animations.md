# Animations & Micro-Interactions

Motion principles and keyframe triggers:

- **Hero Animation:** Subtle ambient background gradient pulse & floating device mockups.
- **Scroll Reveal:** Intersection Observer triggered fade-in-up effect on cards and timeline items.
- **Card Hover:** Smooth 3D tilt & scale transform (`scale(1.02)`) with border glow.
- **Button Hover:** Gradient shimmer overlay & subtle elevation lift.
- **Page Transition:** Smooth section cross-fades without layout shifts.
- **Loading States:** Skeleton screens & spinner indicators for asynchronous JSON fetches.
- **Navbar Effects:** Backdrop filter blur activation on scroll down.
- **Cursor Effects:** Subtle glowing follower dot on desktop screens.
